# COMP1238 Course Notes

## Week 1 - History of Information

- Writing systems allow people to record and share information.
- Cuneiform was an early writing system developed in Mesopotamia.

## Week 2 - Bits and Bytes

- A bit is the smallest unit of digital information and represents 0 or 1.
- A byte contains 8 bits.

## Week 3 - Data and Information

- Data consists of raw facts and figures.
- Information is data organized to provide meaning.

## Week 4 - Computers and Technology

- Hardware refers to the physical parts of a computer.
- Software refers to the programs and instructions a computer runs.

## Week 5 - The Internet and the Web

- The Internet is a global network connecting computers and devices.
- The World Wide Web uses websites and web pages accessed through browsers.
